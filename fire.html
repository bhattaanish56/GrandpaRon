GET PREPARED

Before You Light the Fire

A little preparation makes building a fire safer and more effective. Start with these four essentials before striking your first spark.

01

Prepare the Location

Choose a clear, safe spot away from overhanging branches, dry grass, and other flammable materials.

02

Gather Your Fuel

Collect tinder, kindling, and larger wood in three sizes before you start, keeping everything dry and organized.

03

Choose a Fire-Starting Method

Pick the method that suits your conditions and skill level, from matches and lighters to friction-based techniques.

04

Build the Structure

Arrange your materials in a way that allows air to flow while protecting your initial flame from wind.
