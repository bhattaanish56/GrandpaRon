GET PREPARED

Before You Cast

A little preparation helps you make the most of your time on the water. Start with these four essentials before you cast your first line.

01

Choose the Right Spot

Look for areas with structure, shade, or current where fish are likely to gather and feed.

02

Gather Your Gear

Check your rod, line, hooks, and tackle before heading out, and bring what you need for the conditions.

03

Choose Your Bait

Match your bait or lure to the type of fish you're after and the water you're fishing.

04

Cast Your Line

Practice your casting technique and stay patient as you learn to read the water and adjust your approach.
