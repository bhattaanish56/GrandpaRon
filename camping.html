GET PREPARED

Before You Set Up Camp

A little preparation makes your time outdoors more comfortable and enjoyable. Start with these four essentials before setting up your campsite.

01

Choose Your Campsite

Look for level ground, natural shelter from wind, and a safe distance from water and hazards.

02

Set Up Your Shelter

Pitch your tent or tarp on clear ground, stake it securely, and ensure it's weatherproof before dark.

03

Prepare Your Camp

Organize your gear, set up a cooking area, and store food properly to keep your camp functional and safe.

04

Settle In for the Night

Arrange your sleeping area, secure loose items, and prepare what you'll need for morning before the sun sets.
