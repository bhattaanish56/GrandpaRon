GET PREPARED

Before You Head Out

A little preparation makes time outdoors more rewarding. Start with these four essentials before heading into the field.

01

Know the Land

Familiarize yourself with the terrain, access permissions, and local hunting regulations before you go.

02

Prepare Your Gear

Check your equipment beforehand and pack for the weather, terrain, and time you expect to spend outdoors.

03

Read the Signs

Take time to observe tracks, changes in vegetation, and other signs of wildlife activity.

04

Move with Care

Stay aware of your surroundings, respect wildlife, and move thoughtfully through the landscape.
